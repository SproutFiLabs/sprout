01 / Supply & launch

One billion at creation.

SPROUT launched through the Pons V2 launch factory. The launcher token mints its initial supply to a bonding curve. Its published implementation has no post-deployment mint function; holders can voluntarily burn their own tokens. The token supply is not increased by Sprout’s holder perks or root multipliers.

The Pons V2 launch design graduates from the bonding curve into Uniswap V4 liquidity. Launchpad liquidity and any launchpad buyback or vesting arrangements are separate from Sprout’s optional root lock and Buy & burn feature.

Total supply ≠ circulating supply

A transfer to the dead address removes spendable tokens without reducing the ERC-20 reported total supply. Liquidity, contract balances and other locked holdings must also be considered before describing a circulating supply.

Follow the on-chain record

Use the explorer for current supply, holders and transfers. This page does not claim a verified team allocation, treasury allocation or vesting schedule. A contract balance alone does not establish its beneficiary or unlock conditions.

02 / Holder utility

A little more access.

Holding SPROUT unlocks extra baskets and a weighted voice in votes on the next asset to add. Ordinary holdings qualify after seven days, reduced to 24 hours during the token’s first week. Eligibility uses the lowest sampled wallet balance over the hold window.

Current tier thresholds. Holder baskets are cumulative, in addition to public starter mixes.

Votes concern application features; they do not grant control over anyone’s family vault. Application perks and eligibility settings can change.

03 / Root your SPROUT Live · Optional

Lock tokens. Count for more.

Rooting locks your own SPROUT for a chosen period. It counts toward your tier immediately. Your qualifying amount is eligible wallet holdings plus credit from your locks.

Lock durationTier multiplierExample
30 days1.25×800,000 counts as 1,000,000
90 days1.5×1,000,000 counts as 1,500,000
180 days2×500,000 counts as 1,000,000

No yield, interest or extra tokens. The multiplier changes tier credit only. There is no early withdrawal. On or after the unlock date, the owner can withdraw the original tokens to the same wallet; withdrawal is not automatic. Matured, unwithdrawn locks count at 1×.

The root contract has no owner, admin withdrawal, pause or upgrade mechanism. It has not been independently audited.

Separate eligibility matters: root credit applies to holder tiers, baskets and votes. Intelligence and Harvest currently check actual wallet balances. Moving SPROUT into a root lock may put your wallet below those separate thresholds.

04 / Buy & burn Live · Voluntary

Out of circulation. For good.

Anyone can spend USDG from their own wallet to buy SPROUT on the market and send the purchased tokens directly to 0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD. Nothing is taken from a family’s sprout.

  • Current amounts: $1, $5, $20, or a custom amount up to $100 in USDG.
  • The interface quotes the SPROUT received, includes the displayed pool fee and sets a minimum output. Network fees and price impact also apply.
  • An optional “Add a $1 burn to my buys” setting offers a separate burn after eligible actions. Each transaction still needs the wallet’s approval.
  • Burning does not improve your tier, pay rewards or guarantee any price effect.

The public counter distinguishes burned via Sprout from the full dead-address balance. Tokens sent there outside this feature are not attributed to Sprout’s Buy & burn.

05 / Harvest

Announced rounds. Manual payments.

Harvest is a separate system for discretionary, manually funded distributions. A wallet must hold at least 1,000,000 SPROUT at a published snapshot block and register before the round’s deadline. The announced budget is divided proportionally by actual snapshot holdings among eligible registered wallets, with rounding down.

Each round identifies its payout asset, treasury, budget and deadline. Funds remain in the treasury until the team sends payments; they are not escrowed by registration. The application verifies payment receipts. Root multipliers and SPROUT held in root locks are not included in wallet snapshot balances.

There is no fixed APY, guaranteed payment, recurring payout promise or automatic revenue share. No Harvest rounds are published at this review. Check the live page for subsequent announcements.

06 / Boundaries & sources

Know what you are holding.

SPROUT is separate from USDG and the stock tokens held in family vaults. Holding it does not give a claim on those assets or permission to move them. Rooting is not yield-bearing staking, and burns do not promise appreciation.

Launchpad trading fees are separate from Sprout’s app features. This page does not promise a fixed allocation of trading fees to holders, development or burns. Any separately announced Harvest budget is specific to its round.

Token prices and liquidity can change. A verified source-code match is not an independent audit. Sprout is beta software and its contracts have not been independently audited. Nothing on this page is financial advice.
